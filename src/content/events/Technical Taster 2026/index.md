Welcome to our first technical taster event: the Maze Solving Challenge ⭐️🤖

Join us for a beginner-friendly, hands-on day where you'll build, program, and test your robot against other teams. With a starter kit, you'll assemble a small robot equipped with sensors that lets it navigate its surroundings. Then you'll write the code that brings it to life, teaching your robot how to make decisions and find its way through a mini maze. 

Test, tweak, and try again until your robot reaches the finish line! 🏁🏎️

This is open to beginners and anyone else who wants to explore the different facets of robotics from the assembling to programming to troubleshooting. No prior experience in robotics or programming is needed. Our members will be on hand to guide you through every step.

🔥 Tickets are first come first serve! If we have a lot of interest, we will run another session.
